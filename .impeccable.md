## Design Context

### Users
Two coffee & matcha lovers in Seattle who review cafes together. They use Sips to capture reviews on their phones while at cafes (quick-add flow) and refine them later at a desk. Public visitors browse the site to discover good Seattle cafes — they want honest, personal takes with photos, not Yelp-style reviews. The site is a personal journal that happens to be public.

### Brand Personality
**Warm, indie, personal.** Like a friend's cafe journal — authentic, hand-made, never corporate. The hand-drawn elements (Caveat headings, ink illustrations) are the soul of the brand. It should feel like someone with great taste made this for fun, not for profit.

### Emotional Goals
- **Cozy inspiration** — "I want to grab a coffee and explore Seattle right now"
- **Trust and taste** — "These people know good cafes, I'll follow their picks"

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Ink & Paper"** — modern editorial with hand-drawn ink illustration accents. Confident, not precious. The hand-drawn elements are punctuation, not wallpaper.

- **References:** Mari Kuznetsova (hand-drawn furniture), Pause (tea/coffee illustrations), Camino (editorial wine site), Tilda Wine (structured mono text + organic elements)
- **Anti-references:** Yelp, generic restaurant review sites, anything with star ratings and blue/white corporate UI, overly sketchy/scratchy illustration styles
- **Theme:** Light mode only (cream background). No dark mode planned.

### Typography
- **Headings:** Caveat — hand-drawn, elegant, not elongated
- **Display/Accent:** Instrument Serif — editorial serif for pull quotes, subtitles
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans — warm geometric sans-serif
- **Metadata:** IBM Plex Mono — uppercase, letterspaced, structured contrast

### Color Palette
- **Cream** `#FFF8F0` — background
- **Stone** `#F0EBE3` — secondary background, cards
- **Espresso** `#2C2420` — primary text
- **Espresso Muted** `#7A6E66` — secondary text
- **Terracotta** `#C4735B` — primary action, CTA
- **Sage** `#8BAA8F` — secondary accent
- **Ink Blue** `#3D5A80` — illustrations, decorative
- **Pink** `#E8B4B8` — highlight, selection

### Illustration Style
Clean confident ink strokes generated via Recraft AI. Hand-drawn but precise — uniform line weight, smooth curves, no sketchy marks. Single-color (black ink on transparent). Used sparingly for hero moments, empty states, section accents.

### Design Principles

1. **Hand-drawn soul, editorial structure.** Caveat headings and ink illustrations give warmth. IBM Plex Mono metadata and clean card layouts give structure. The tension between organic and structured is what makes Sips feel distinctive.

2. **Restraint over decoration.** Use hand-drawn elements as accents, not everywhere. A single ink illustration on a page is charming; ten is cluttered. Generous whitespace lets the content breathe.

3. **Content first, chrome second.** Cafe names, ratings, photos, and reviews are the star. UI should frame content, not compete with it. No flashy animations or attention-grabbing UI patterns.

4. **Mobile is the creation surface.** Reviews are captured on phones at cafes. Touch targets must be generous (48px+), flows must be fast, and the camera is a first-class input.

5. **Warm, not cute.** The aesthetic is confident and tasteful, not precious or infantile. Think "indie magazine" not "kawaii sticker book." Terracotta and espresso tones, not pastels and bubble shapes.

### Accessibility
- WCAG AA compliance (contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, screen reader support)
- Paper texture overlay kept at very low opacity (0.03) to avoid readability issues
- Focus states visible and consistent
- Alt text on all illustrations
